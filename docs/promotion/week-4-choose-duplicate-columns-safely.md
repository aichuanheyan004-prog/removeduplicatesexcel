# How to Choose Columns for Excel Duplicate Removal

> Week 4 publication record. Published URL: https://www.removeduplicatesexcel.org/blog/choose-duplicate-columns-safely/

## Search task

Primary cluster: choose columns to remove duplicates in Excel, duplicate key, define duplicate rows. Intent: avoid deleting legitimate records by defining identity before cleanup.

## What the article solves

It compares one-column, multi-column, and all-relevant-column keys, gives a synthetic table example, and requires preview and export validation.

## Distribution plan

- Publish the canonical URL in the Blog index and sitemap.
- Use it in spreadsheet-cleanup or Excel-help answers where column selection is the actual problem; disclose the site relationship.
- Use only synthetic tables in examples. Do not upload or reproduce customer workbooks.
- Track impressions, tool starts, preview completions, verified exports, and error reasons without collecting workbook contents.

## Editorial boundary

The tool is for authorized XLSX/CSV cleanup and exports displayed-value snapshots with documented feature limits. It does not bypass workbook passwords or preserve every macro, formula, style, or chart.
